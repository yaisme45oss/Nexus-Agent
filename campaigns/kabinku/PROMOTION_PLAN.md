```
AWARENESS  →  The 27s reel (9:16 + 4:5), crane-lift / installation clips, project reveals

              project carousels, interior tours, "spec explained" posts, testimonials,
              "price from RM…" posts per model
CONVERT    →  Click-to-WhatsApp ads ("Minta sebut harga terperinci")

ADVOCACY   →  Google reviews from past clients, referral reward, project handover videos
```

## 4. Channels and tactics

### 4.1 Meta (Facebook + Instagram): main lead engine
- **Campaign:** Engagement → *Messaging apps (WhatsApp)*, Advantage+ placements. Use the 9:16 reel for Reels/Stories and the 4:5 cut for Feed.
- **Ad sets:** (a) Homestay/landowners, (b) Business/SME, (c) Contractors/site office; each with interest suggestions (homestay, Airbnb, real estate investing, small business owners, construction) and Malaysia geo weighted to the states above.
- **Creative test (first 2 weeks):** 3 hooks × same body. Keep the winner, replace losers weekly.
  - A. *"Nak bina homestay? pejabat? sekolah?"* (current reel)
  - B. Crane lowering a cabin into place, no text for the first 1.5s (needs KabinKu's own footage)
  - C. *"Berapa kos sebenar kabin 20 kaki?"* with a real "harga dari RM…" (needs KabinKu's price)
- **Retargeting:** 50% video viewers + page/IG engagers (30 days) → carousel of 8 projects + testimonial video.
- **Instant Form variant** (if WhatsApp volume is too low-quality): questions copied from their own order form (type, purpose, budget band, site location, appointment date).

### 4.2 TikTok
- Post 3×/week from the existing account (@kabinkucabincontainer). Formats: crane-lift and installation timelapses, "inside a 20ft cabin" tours, Q&A replies ("boleh pasang atas tanah kampung?"), team behind-the-scenes at the Klang yard.
- Boost the top organic post each fortnight with **Spark Ads** to the same WhatsApp CTA.

### 4.3 Google Search + Business Profile
- **Search ads** on high-intent Malay and English terms. Keyword themes to test (search volumes not checked yet): *rumah kabin, harga rumah kabin, kabin pejabat, kabin pejabat tapak, rumah kontena, kabin homestay, kabin modular, pondok pengawal, modular house malaysia, cabin container price malaysia, site office cabin, portable cabin selangor*. Add negatives for *sewa* (rent) if they only sell, and for jobs/careers.
- **Google Business Profile** for the Klang showroom: 30+ project photos, weekly posts, product listings with "from" prices, and a push to collect reviews from the 25 featured project clients.

### 4.4 Marketplaces
- List the Basic and Pitch Roof models on **Mudah.my**, **Facebook Marketplace** and **Carousell** with a clear "harga dari" and WhatsApp number. Cabin buyers compare prices here first.

### 4.5 B2B and government
- Use the MOF Bumiputera + CIDB G3 status: keep the eP-Perolehan vendor profile current, register as a vendor with GLCs and large contractors, and send a one-page capability PDF (projects, certifications, standard spec) to contractors' purchasing teams.
- WhatsApp/LinkedIn outreach to project managers of active construction sites in Klang Valley for site offices and workers' quarters.

### 4.6 Referrals and events
- Referral reward for past clients and contractors (amount for KabinKu to decide), tracked by a code in the WhatsApp greeting.
- Monthly "Hari Terbuka Showroom" at Klang, promoted with a Meta event ad to a 30 km radius. Consider a booth at home/building expos (check HOMEDEC / ARCHIDEX dates).

## 5. Website fixes before spending on ads

1. **Projects page** ends with "PAGE IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION": remove it.
2. **Add prices.** A "harga dari RM…" per model (Basic, Pitch Roof, 2-storey) filters out bad leads and lifts ad CTR.
3. **One email address.** The site shows info@kabinku.com, kabinku@gmail.com, and the domain is kabinku.com.my. Pick one on the .com.my domain.
4. **Tracking.** Add Meta Pixel + Conversions API, GA4, and UTM-tagged WhatsApp links (`?text=` prefill per campaign) so each lead is attributed.
5. **Testimonials and Google reviews** on the home page; a short case study per featured project (location, units, time to install, quote band).
6. **Language.** Home is in Malay, Products in English. Keep Malay as primary with an English toggle.
7. Footer still says © 2025.

## 6. Budget (suggested starting split, adjust after week 2)

| Item | Starter / month | Growth / month |
|---|---|---|
| Meta click-to-WhatsApp + retargeting | RM1,800 (≈RM60/day) | RM4,500 |
| Google Search | RM900 (≈RM30/day) | RM2,500 |
| TikTok Spark Ads | RM300 | RM1,000 |
| **Total ad spend** | **RM3,000** | **RM8,000** |

**How to judge cost per lead:** work back from margin. Example with made-up numbers: average job RM45,000 × 20% gross margin = RM9,000 per sale. If 1 in 15 qualified WhatsApp leads buys, a qualified lead is worth up to RM600 before profit. Replace these with KabinKu's real average job value, margin and close rate.

| Lead | Cost per WhatsApp conversation | set after week 2 baseline |
